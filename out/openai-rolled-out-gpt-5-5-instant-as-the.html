GPT-5.5 Instant Is Now ChatGPT's Default Model for Everyone

OpenAI swapped in GPT-5.5 Instant on May 5, 2026, promising 52.5% fewer hallucinations on high-stakes prompts and more concise replies for hundreds of millions of users.

Topic page · generated Jun 12, 2026 6 sources

On May 5, 2026, OpenAI replaced GPT-5.3 Instant with GPT-5.5 Instant as the default model across all ChatGPT tiers — Free, Go, Plus, Pro, Business, Enterprise, and Edu — requiring no action from users. The model targets reliability over raw capability: OpenAI's internal benchmarks show a 52.5% drop in hallucinations on high-stakes medical, legal, and financial prompts, and a 37.3% reduction in inaccurate claims on conversations previously flagged by users. The rollout also introduced memory-source visibility and enhanced personalization from past chats, files, and Gmail. Community reaction has been mixed, with factual-task users praising the upgrade while creative writers report the new model feels more conservative and hedging.

Why it matters

GPT-5.5 Instant is the model that hundreds of millions of everyday ChatGPT users encounter without ever choosing a model — making it the de facto face of consumer AI for most people. Cutting hallucinations on medical, legal, and financial queries by more than half is a meaningful trust signal as AI moves from novelty to daily production tool in workplaces.
