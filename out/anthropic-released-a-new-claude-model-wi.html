Where things stand

Anthropic launched Claude Fable 5 and Claude Mythos 5 on June 9, 2026, marking the first public release of a Mythos-class model. Fable 5 is available to Pro, Max, Team, and Enterprise users and tops nearly every benchmark Anthropic tested, while Mythos 5 — sharing the same underlying model but with fewer safety restrictions — is reserved for roughly 200 approved institutions via Project Glasswing. On the SWE-Bench Pro software engineering benchmark, Fable 5 scores 80.3%, well ahead of rivals GPT-5.5 (58.6%) and Gemini 3.1 Pro (54.2%). Both models are priced at $10 per million input tokens and $50 per million output tokens — less than half the cost of Claude Mythos Preview.

Availability
Fable 5 is available immediately on Claude.ai Pro, Max, Team, and Enterprise plans, and via the Claude API. Mythos 5 is restricted to ~200 Project Glasswing partner institutions in collaboration with the US government; a broader Trusted Access Program is planned. The bigger picture

Fable 5 and Mythos 5 represent a new performance tier that Anthropic claims surpasses all of its prior generally available models and key rivals in coding, scientific reasoning, and vision — with real-world validation from companies like Stripe and IMC. The dual-model strategy, separating a safety-guardrailed public model from a restricted, higher-capability research model, sets a precedent for how frontier AI labs might responsibly release increasingly powerful systems. Mythos 5's autonomous drug design and genomics results, if independently validated, could mark a concrete step toward AI-accelerated scientific discovery.
