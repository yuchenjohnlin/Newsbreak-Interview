Bulgaria Wins Eurovision 2026 in Vienna with Record-Breaking Margin

DARA's "Bangaranga" triumphed at the 70th Eurovision Song Contest, held at the Wiener Stadthalle, giving Bulgaria its first-ever victory by the largest winning margin in the contest's history.

The 70th Eurovision Song Contest took place in Vienna, Austria, from May 12–16, 2026, at the Wiener Stadthalle — the city's third time hosting the event. Bulgaria's DARA won the Grand Final on May 16 with "Bangaranga," topping both the jury vote and televote by a record 173-point margin over the second-place entry. The contest featured 35 participating countries, with five nations (Iceland, Ireland, the Netherlands, Slovenia, and Spain) boycotting over Israel's inclusion. Romania finished third, equaling its best-ever placement, while Australia and Italy rounded out the top five.

Reader value beyond the headline
The 70th anniversary edition was a landmark moment for the contest, producing Bulgaria's first-ever winner and the highest winning margin in Eurovision history. Five countries staged the largest boycott since 1970 over Israel's participation, reflecting how the geopolitical tensions around the Gaza conflict have increasingly spilled into the contest. The record sell-out speed and Vienna's city-wide programming underline Eurovision's growing status as one of the world's biggest live entertainment events.
